Effective October 25, 2026

This policy explains what information Income to Assets ("we," "us") collects when you visit myincometoassets.com (the "Site"), how we use it, and the choices you have. The Site is an informational page that points visitors to our community on Skool. It has no accounts, forms, or checkout.

What we collect

We do not ask you for your name, email address, or any other personal details on this Site. The only information collected is visitor activity data gathered by Google Analytics, and only if you accept analytics cookies:

  • Pages you view and how long you stay
  • Clicks on links that take you to our Skool page
  • The site or search that referred you
  • Your device type, browser, operating system, and approximate location (derived from your IP address)
  • A random identifier stored in a cookie so repeat visits can be recognized

How we use it

We use this information only to understand how many people visit the Site and how many click through to our Skool community, so we can improve the Site. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Cookies and your choice

Analytics cookies (named like _ga) are off by default. They are set only after you click Accept on the cookie notice. If you click Decline, or ignore the notice, we do not set analytics cookies. Google may still receive limited, cookie-free signals about the visit, such as that a page was loaded.

Do Not Track

The Site does not currently respond to browser "Do Not Track" signals. Analytics cookies are off unless you accept them.

Retention

Analytics data is kept according to the retention period we set in Google Analytics, after which Google deletes it automatically.
